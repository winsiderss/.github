## About Winsider

Winsider Seminars & Solutions, Inc. was established in 2009 by [Alex Ionescu](https://github.com/ionescu007) as a spiritual successor to David Solomon Expert Seminars, the first and only original Windows Internals training company, co-founded by industry veterans David Solomon and Mark Russinovich. Building on the original training catalogue that David entrusted to Alex, Winsider continues to teach industry partners worldwide across enterprise and government, and offers public courses at leading cybersecurity conferences.

Winsider also continues its involvement in the [Windows Internals book](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/resources/windows-internals) series, including its 7th Edition, and develops key Windows tools used by experts worldwide, such as the award-winning System Informer system utility, previously known as Process Hacker. Additionally, Winsider works closely as a consultant with multiple industry partners in the areas of system design and operating system security.

- **[System Informer](https://github.com/winsiderss/systeminformer)** — Monitor system resources, inspect processes, investigate software, and debug Windows applications.
- **[VisualUEFI](https://github.com/ionescu007/VisualUefi)** — Develop UEFI applications and drivers with EDK-II and Visual Studio.
- **[SimpleVisor](https://github.com/ionescu007/SimpleVisor)** — Explore Intel VT-x virtualization with a small, documented hypervisor for Windows and UEFI.
- **[SpecuCheck](https://github.com/ionescu007/SpecuCheck)** — Inspect hardware and software mitigations for Meltdown, Spectre, and related speculative execution vulnerabilities.
- **[PoolViewer](https://github.com/yardenshafir/PoolViewer)** — View and filter Windows pool allocations from memory dumps, with PoolViewerExt providing pool address and tag inspection in WinDbg.
